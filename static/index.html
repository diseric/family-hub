Connect Google Calendar

Sync work, personal, and family events continuously with live updates.
💡 How to get your Google Calendar Secret iCal Address:
  1. On your computer or laptop, go to calendar.google.com.
  2. In the left panel under "My calendars", hover over the calendar you want to sync and click the three dots ⋮ > Settings and sharing.
  3. Scroll down to the "Integrate calendar" section.
  4. Copy the link labeled "Secret address in iCal format" (or Public address if public).
Format example: https://calendar.google.com/calendar/ical/user%40gmail.com/private-xxxxxx/basic.ics
